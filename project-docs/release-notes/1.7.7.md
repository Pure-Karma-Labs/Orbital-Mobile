## What's New (draft copy)

### App Store (limit 4000 characters)

```
Keep a copy on your phone
• Open a photo, video or file full screen and tap Save to keep a copy of it on this phone.
• Save whichever items you want, one at a time.
• Or open Settings > File Library, narrow it down with the filters, and tap Save all to keep a copy of everything it is showing.
• Orbital tells you how many items and roughly how much space before it starts, and you can stop part-way.
• The original stays in Orbital — saving makes a copy.
• The first time you save something, Orbital explains what a saved copy means before it saves anything.

Also in this update
• Chats are listed by most recent activity.
• People you've blocked are hidden from Chats and New Chat until you unblock them.
```

### Play Console (limit 500 characters)

```
• Tap Save on any photo, video or file to keep a copy on your device
• Or open Settings > File Library and tap Save all to copy everything the filters show
• The original stays in Orbital — saving makes a copy
• Chats are listed by most recent activity
• People you've blocked are hidden from Chats and New Chat
```

## Internal notes

- **Sources for the copy:** #878 — PR 1 (per-item Save in the media lightbox,
  drafted 2026-10-09) and PR 2 (bulk "Save all" from File Library, drafted
  2026-10-13). Drafted from the issue; device-verified on iOS and Android in
  the 2026-10-20 cut smoke (per-item Save, Save all with a matching count, and
  the first-save disclosure). Re-check on the submitted build before pasting.
- **App Review note (App Store Review / Play review):** Save lives as a Save
  button in the full-screen media viewer, reached by tapping any photo, video or
  file in a post or in Settings > File Library. Saving writes a copy of that one
  item to the device (iOS: the photo library, or Files; Android: the Pictures,
  Movies or Download folder); the original stays in the app. Before the first
  save, a one-time disclosure alert explains that saved copies leave end-to-end
  encryption, may be backed up to the reviewer's own cloud service, and stay on
  the device after logging out or deleting the account. The reviewer can post a
  photo from the demo account and then save it to exercise the whole path.
- **App Review note, "Save all" (added 2026-10-13):** Settings > File Library
  has a Save all button in its header. It saves everything matching the type
  and orbit filters currently showing — not just the visible page — after a
  confirmation alert stating the item count and approximate size. A run shows a
  progress row with a stop control, pauses if the app is backgrounded, and ends
  in a dismissible summary. On iOS, documents are collected into a single Files
  picker at the end of the run rather than one picker per file.
- **Deliberately not in the copy:**
  - The item ceiling on one run, the free-space precheck, and the fact that a
    blocked member's media and media from orbits you have left are skipped.
    All three are correct behaviour with no sentence a user needs.
  - The storage permission scoping, staging-directory cleanup and wipe wiring
    that ship alongside Save — plumbing with nothing user-visible to describe.
- **Added at the 1.7.7 cut (2026-10-20, Alex-approved):** "Chats are listed by
  most recent activity" (#883/#884) and "People you've blocked are hidden from
  Chats and New Chat" (#908/#909) — both verified on device in earlier smoke
  sessions. The Play copy was rewritten to fit 500 characters, dropping the
  count/space and first-save-disclosure lines (both stay in the App Store copy).
- **Smoke status at the cut:** the 2026-10-20 local-stack smoke was green on
  iOS + Android (tester1/tester2: signup, cross-platform joins, E2EE, search,
  DMs + images, multi-chunk video, playback, persistence, invites, #941 DM-cache
  survival with the backend down, #878 save-to-device). Not device-verified at
  the cut, owed on the TestFlight / internal-track build: the Android API 28
  legacy save path, the low-storage block, the App Store review-notes re-check,
  and #938's 24h bound / #942's refresh re-classification (unit-tested only).
- **Deliberately not announced at the cut:** the group-key self-heal, its 24h
  bound and refresh behaviour (#915, #938, #942 — describing them would describe

  Block/Report touch target (#888) — plumbing with nothing user-visible to say;
  clearer sign-in/invite error messages (#865/#873/#874) — held out because it
  was not explicitly confirmed on both platforms at the cut.
